## The scope

Designing a complete **smart home network** simulation in **Cisco Packet
Tracer**: connected devices, a local gateway and cloud services downstream.

## The hardening

A connected home is a textbook attack surface — many devices, little compute,
and constant communication outwards. The work focused on:

- **robust security configurations** on the networking equipment;
- **access controls** limiting what each device is allowed to reach;
- **protecting data transmission** towards the cloud services;
- hardening the network against **unauthorised access**.

## Why it is useful

Simulating rather than deploying means you can **get it wrong without
consequence**, and see the effect of a filtering rule or a segmentation mistake
immediately. This is the project that gave me the segmentation instincts I have
used since.
